Mercy walks 4 kilometres to school every morning before sunrise, carrying a torn bag held together with string. Mercy's school sent her home last term because her fees were unpaid — but she still shows up every morning and waits outside the gate, hoping someone will let her in. She wants to be a doctor.

Amara often goes to school without breakfast, sometimes without shoes. His teacher says he is the brightest child in the class — he just needs one meal a day and someone to believe in him.

🇹🇿 Tanzania Medical Care
Zawadi Kimani
Age 11 · Dar es Salaam

Zawadi was born with a treatable heart condition that her family cannot afford to treat. Her mother, a single parent of five, cleans offices at night and sells mandazi in the morning. Zawadi's condition means she tires easily, but she still tries to help her mother every day before school. The surgery she needs costs what her family earns in three years. She smiles every single day.

🇺🇬 Uganda Education
Emmanuel Okello
Age 13 · Kampala

Emmanuel lost both parents to illness when he was eight. He has been living at a children's home in Kampala ever since, sharing a room with eleven other boys. Despite everything, Emmanuel has topped his class in mathematics for three consecutive years. His dream is to build a school in his village. He needs a scholarship to continue his secondary education — without it, he will have to leave school next term.

🇷🇼 Rwanda Nutrition
Aline Uwimana
Age 6 · Kigali

Aline was brought to the care centre severely malnourished at age four. Today, after two years of support, she has gained weight and started school. But the centre that saved her life is running out of funding. Thirty-two children like Aline depend on this centre for their daily meal — the only guaranteed food they will eat today. Without continued support, the centre will close its doors by the end of this month.

🇧🇮 Burundi Clean Water
Pierre Nkurunziza
Age 10 · Bujumbura

Pierre's village has no clean water source. Every day, he and his younger sister walk 6 kilometres to collect water from a river shared with livestock. The water makes them sick, but it is all they have. Pierre has missed 40 days of school this year alone due to waterborne illness. A borehole for his entire village of 200 people costs what 2,000 people giving one bob each would raise. That is all it takes.
