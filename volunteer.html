You do not need money to change a life. You can give your time, your skills, and your presence. Every volunteer hour is worth a hundred bobs to the children we serve.

Open Volunteer Roles

Find your place with us

📖
Reading Volunteer
🇰🇪 Kenya · 🇺🇬 Uganda · 🇷🇼 Rwanda
Read with children at care centres and schools. Help them improve their literacy one story at a time. No experience needed — just patience and warmth.
2–4 hrs/week · In person
💻
Digital & Social Media
🌍 Remote · All Countries
Help us tell our story online. Create content, manage social media, write posts, and help spread the word about One Bob Hope to new audiences.
3–5 hrs/week · Remote
🍽️
Feeding Programme Helper
🇰🇪 Nairobi · 🇹🇿 Dar es Salaam
Help prepare and serve meals at our partner feeding centres. Be part of the team that ensures every child eats today.
Weekend mornings · In person
🏗️
Construction & Repair
🇧🇮 Burundi · 🇪🇹 Ethiopia
Help build and repair classrooms, boreholes, and shelter. No professional experience required — just willingness to get your hands dirty for a good cause.
One-off or regular · In person
🎨
Arts & Creative
🌍 Remote or In Person
Teach drawing, music, drama, or crafts to children. Help us capture their stories through photography or video. Creativity heals and inspires.
Flexible · Remote or In person
🏥
Health & Wellness
🇷🇼 Rwanda · 🇺🇬 Uganda
Medical and nursing professionals who can offer health screenings, basic care, and health education at our partner centres. All qualifications welcome.
Monthly visits · In person
