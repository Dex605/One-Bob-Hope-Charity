One Bob Hope Charity

"Bob" is what we all call the smallest coin in our pocket —
no matter which country you're from in East Africa.
One bob from a million hearts changes everything.

Why "One Bob"? — We never ask how much you can give. We only ask that you give what you can. One bob from you. One bob from your neighbour. One bob from a stranger three countries away. Together, a million bobs build schools, feed children, and restore futures that poverty tried to steal.

Our Mission
To mobilize everyday people across East Africa to collectively end childhood poverty — one voluntary contribution at a time, no matter how small.
Our Vision
A generation of East African children who grow up with full stomachs, warm shelter, quality education, and the freedom to dream.
