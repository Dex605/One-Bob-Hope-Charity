One Bob Hope Charity was not born in a boardroom. It was born at a roadside, watching a child walk to school in torn shoes, and asking — what if everyone around me gave just one bob?

The Name

Why "One Bob"?

"Bob" is what we all call the smallest coin in our pocket — no matter which country you are from in East Africa. In Kenya it is a shilling. In Ethiopia it is a birr. In Rwanda it is a franc. But everywhere, it is a bob. Small. Overlooked. Underestimated.

We chose this name because we believe the most powerful force for change in East Africa is not a government programme or a foreign aid package. It is ordinary people choosing to give their one bob — consistently, voluntarily, and without being asked twice.

One bob from you. One bob from your neighbour. One bob from a stranger three countries away. Together, a million bobs build schools, feed children, fund surgeries, and restore futures that poverty tried to steal. We never ask how much. We only ask that you give what you can.

🎯
Our Mission
What we do every day
To mobilize everyday people across East Africa and beyond to collectively end childhood poverty — one voluntary contribution at a time, no matter how small. We connect willing hearts to real children with real needs, transparently, efficiently, and with dignity.
🌅
Our Vision
What we are working toward
A generation of East African children who grow up with full stomachs, warm shelter, quality education, and the freedom to dream — funded by the collective generosity of their own people. A continent that saves itself, one bob at a time.
Our Core Values

The principles that
guide everything we do

🤲
Dignity First
Every child we serve is shown with respect and told with dignity. We do not exploit suffering to attract donations. We tell true stories with permission and with honour.
🔍
Full Transparency
Every shilling donated is accounted for. We publish detailed breakdowns of how funds are used. Donors can request a full financial report at any time.
🤝
Collective Power
We do not wait for the wealthy. We believe that millions of ordinary people giving one bob each are more powerful than a handful of large donors. Collective action is our model.
🌱
Long-term Change
We do not just feed children for a day. We fund education, infrastructure, and community systems that create lasting change. We are here for a generation, not a moment.
🌍
East African Identity
This charity is of East Africa, for East Africa. We are not a foreign aid project. We are East Africans helping East Africans — and we are proud of that.
💬
Community Voice
The communities we serve have a say in how funds are used. We listen before we act. We ask before we give. The people closest to the problem know the solution best.
