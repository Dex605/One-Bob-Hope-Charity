Words from the people
your bobs reached

These are not written by us. These are the words of mothers, children, teachers, and community leaders whose lives were touched by ordinary people giving what they could.

"I am twelve years old. Before, I used to sleep hungry every night. Now I eat breakfast before school and I can think clearly in class. My teacher says I am improving every week. I want to say thank you to every person who gave even one bob. It reached me. I felt it. Someone I have never met decided I was worth one bob — and that one bob told me I matter."
FT
Fatuma Twalib
Student, Age 12
🇰🇪 Mombasa, Kenya

Transformation Stories

Before and after
your one bob arrived

Emmanuel Okello
Age 13 · Kampala, Uganda 🇺🇬
Before
Facing dropout at end of term. No fees. Sharing one school uniform with another boy on alternate days. Going to bed hungry 4 nights a week. Crying silently so the other boys in the home don't hear.
After
Fees paid. Full scholarship secured. Own uniform. Eating three meals daily. Top of mathematics class. Applying for secondary school. Smiling in every photo. Telling his teacher he will build a school one day.
Pierre's Village
Bujumbura Rural, Burundi 🇧🇮
Before
200 people sharing a contaminated river. Children missing school weekly due to waterborne illness. Women waking at 4am to collect water. Crops failing from poor irrigation. Hospital visits consuming family savings.
After
Borehole drilled and operational. Clean water 200 metres from every home. School attendance up 80%. Hospital visits down by half. Women now have mornings free for small businesses. Hope restored to an entire community.
Aline Uwimana
Age 6 · Kigali, Rwanda 🇷🇼
Before
Severely malnourished at age four. Weight of a two-year-old. Could barely stand or walk. Brought to the care centre by a neighbour. Mother working three jobs and still unable to feed her.
After
Healthy weight. Running and playing. Started school this term. Favourite subject is drawing. Wants to be an artist. Sings in the morning. Her mother says she is "like a different child — like the child God intended her to be."
Juma's School
Dodoma, Tanzania 🇹🇿
Before
340 students, 2 teachers, collapsed roof. One book shared between six children. 60% attendance. Children writing in dirt. Teacher unpaid for 3 months but still showing up every day.
After
Roof repaired. 400 new textbooks. Two additional teachers funded. Attendance at 94%. Teacher salary supported for one year. Children now have their own exercise books. Juma came first in district exams.
